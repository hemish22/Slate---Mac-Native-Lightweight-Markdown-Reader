# A little space to think.

Good ideas deserve a clear view. **Slate** gives your Markdown room to breathe, whether you are reading, writing, or doing a little of both.

## One document. Three views.

Switch between the source and the finished page, or keep them side by side.

| View | Shortcut | Make room for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Editor | ⌘1 | Your next draft |
| Preview | ⌘2 | A comfortable read |
| Split | ⌘3 | Both at once |

## The small things, considered.

- A native editor with line numbers and syntax colors.
- A live preview that takes shape as you type.
- Plain `.md` files that stay right where you put them.

> Less interface. More room for your words.
